# Furious Squirrels visual review rubric

Ten categories, each scored 0–10 in half points against the captures only. **Gates:** every category ≥ 7, except
Hot Springs authenticity, which gates at ≥ 6. Total out of 100.

| # | Category (key) | 10 looks like | 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Art direction (`art`) | one coherent storybook style across backdrop, terrain, trees, squirrels, nuts and UI | parts look like they come from different games |
| 2 | Hot Springs authenticity (`place`) | Bathhouse Row is recognizable (true order, dome, towers, magnolias, lamps, steam, forested mountains); seasons feel like the Ouachitas | generic town and generic hills |
| 3 | Character appeal (`chars`) | the squirrels are cute, expressive, and the two families read apart by shape at play size | stiff or muddy figures, families confusable |
| 4 | Animation and juice (`juice`) | throws, hits, branch breaks, tumbles and knockouts feel lively and readable | static frames, effects too small to notice |
| 5 | Battlefield readability (`read`) | you can see who's whose, who's up, where the nut is going, wind and steam at a glance | squirrels, nuts or targets get lost in the scenery |
| 6 | HUD and menus (`ui`) | clean, consistent, park-sign styled, nothing overlaps, clear hierarchy | cluttered, inconsistent or overlapping |
| 7 | Typography and text (`type`) | legible at every size, consistent families and weights, good contrast | cramped, tiny or low-contrast text |
| 8 | Mobile layout (`mobile`) | portrait play is comfortable: big targets, the action framed, nothing cut off | cramped or clipped, action off screen |
| 9 | Ground and foreground (`ground`) | terrain, craters, trees and leaf piles look finished and integrated with the backdrop | flat fills, seams, placeholder look |
| 10 | Overall polish and charm (`polish`) | feels like a finished, wholesome family game you'd want to play | unfinished prototype |

Scores are judged against shipped 2D casual games of this kind (cartoon artillery and physics-puzzle games), not
against other Hot Springs Games titles.
