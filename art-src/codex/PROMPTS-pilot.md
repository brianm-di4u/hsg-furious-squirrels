# Furious Squirrels: Codex backdrop pilot

Rules (shared with `games/trivia/art-src/PROMPTS.md`): no text or lettering, no logos or signs with words, no people,
no living-artist styles, family friendly. Real buildings are drawn as architecture only.

## Pilot: `art-src/codex/bd-middle-summer-day.png`
Composition reference: `art-src/blender/renders/guide-middle.png` (2500×1000 Blender block-out, transparent sky). The
painting must keep the guide's layout: the same buildings in the same left-to-right order and positions, the street
line at 70% of the height, the forested hillside behind, magnolias and five-globe lamp posts along the curb, the road
across the bottom.

Prompt:

> A wide storybook panorama painting of a historic row of early-1900s bathhouses at the foot of a forested Ozark
> mountain, seen straight on from across the street, matching the reference image's composition exactly (same
> buildings, order, positions and proportions). Left to right: a flat-roofed tan brick bathhouse with green round
> medallions; a white stucco bathhouse under a big red-tile hip roof; a white bathhouse with green-tile roofs and a
> glazed centre; a light-yellow brick bathhouse with dark brown diamond patterns, two small red hipped end pavilions
> and a copper-and-glass entrance canopy; the widest white bathhouse with red tile roofs and a large dome tiled in
> buff, ochre, turquoise and blue chevron stripes topped by a small bronze lantern; a white bathhouse with twin
> three-tier towers capped in red tile; a cream bathhouse with a row of seven columns; a plain white stucco bathhouse
> with a low pediment; a small white building with a red hip roof. A lawn and a single row of dark glossy magnolia
> trees with white blossoms and dark green five-globe lamp posts line the curb; a gray street with a dashed yellow
> centre line runs across the bottom. Wisps of warm spring steam drift up from the hillside behind the buildings. The
> mountain behind is a lush summer mix of round oak and hickory crowns and pointed shortleaf pines in many greens.
> Cheerful cartoon storybook style, soft gouache textures, clean shapes, warm midday sunlight from the upper left,
> gentle shadows, saturated but natural colours. The sky above the mountain is a perfectly flat pure white (#FFFFFF)
> with no clouds, so it can be keyed out. No text, no signs with lettering, no people, no cars, no animals, no tower.

Output size: 1536×1024. The panorama fills the full width; the hill top may reach near the top edge. The game crops
a 2.5:1 band (1536×614) whose bottom is about 18% below the street line, and keys the white sky to transparent.
