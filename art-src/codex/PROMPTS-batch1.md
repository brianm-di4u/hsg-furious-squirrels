# Furious Squirrels: Codex backdrop batch 1

Rules: no text or lettering, no logos, no people, no cars, no animals, no tower, no living-artist styles; family
friendly. Style reference for every image: the approved pilot `art-src/codex/bd-middle-summer-day.png` (same
storybook gouache look, same scale, the same flat pure white #FFFFFF sky so it can be keyed out, the curb/street line
at about 76% of the height (≈780 px of 1024), the road across the bottom, a single row of magnolias with five-globe
lamp posts along the curb). Every output is 1536×1024.

## A. New stretches (paint from the Blender guide, in the pilot's style)
1. `bd-north-summer-day.png` from `art-src/blender/renders/guide-north.png`: the north end. Left to right: the large
   yellow-brick Arlington Hotel with twin towers set back on a lawn terrace (partly cut off at the left edge), a lawn
   gap, the flat-roofed tan-brick Superior with green round medallions, the white Hale under a big red-tile hip roof,
   the white Maurice with green-tile roofs and a glazed centre, then the light-yellow Fordyce with brown diamond
   patterns and the start of the domed Quapaw at the right edge. Hale is centred.
2. `bd-south-spring-day.png` from `art-src/blender/renders/guide-south.png`: the south end, SPRING. Left to right: the
   end of the domed Quapaw, the white Ozark with twin three-tier towers, the cream Buckstaff with seven columns
   (centred), the plain white Lamar with a low pediment, the small white Administration Building with a red hip roof,
   then hillside forest. Spring: fresh light-green leaf-out, pink-purple redbud and white flowering dogwood scattered
   in the hillside forest, magnolias with white blossoms.
