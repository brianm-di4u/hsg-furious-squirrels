# Furious Squirrels: terrain texture

Rules: no text, no logos, no people, no animals, no living-artist styles.

## `art-src/codex/tex-soil.png` (1024×1024, seamless tile)
Style reference: `art-src/codex/bd-middle-summer-day.png` (storybook gouache, warm, clean shapes).

> A seamless, tileable texture of a cross-section of Ozark mountain soil seen from the side, painted in a cheerful
> storybook gouache style: warm brown earth with soft horizontal layering, small rounded pebbles and a few flat
> sandstone and novaculite-white stones, thin pale roots, a few buried acorn caps, gentle dappled shading. Evenly lit,
> frameless, no horizon, no sky, no grass, no strong focal point, no text. Colours: browns around #7A5A3A and #5E4530
> with tan #A08A6A stones. It must tile seamlessly left-right and top-bottom.
